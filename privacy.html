Privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Symlinker does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

  • Links you create. Symlinker creates links by running the Windows mklink command on your computer. The paths you type or drop into the app stay on your computer.
  • Administrator rights. The app asks for administrator rights because Windows requires them to create symbolic links. It uses them only to run mklink.
  • Nothing saved. The app does not keep settings, history or logs.
  • Updates. The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Microsoft Store, under Microsoft's privacy statement. The ClickOnce version checks this website for new versions, and GitHub hosts it under the GitHub privacy statement. The portable version never checks for updates.
  • No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. The app makes no network connections of its own.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
